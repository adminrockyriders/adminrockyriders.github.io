Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Rocky Riders is the club app of the Castlemaine Rocky Riders mountain bike club (“the club”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Contact for anything in this policy: adminrockyriders@gmail.com

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a display name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and name with us; we do not receive your Google password or any other Google account data. If you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and an email address — which may be a private relay address Apple provides on your behalf — and nothing else; we do not receive your Apple password or other Apple account data.

Rider profile. Club organisers maintain a rider list for running races: name, gender, age, riding category, bib number, and whether photo consent has been given. You can link your account to your rider profile so the app can show you your own results.

Race records. The app’s purpose is race timing, so it stores race results, lap times and checkpoint crossing times, together with the rider they belong to and, for checkpoint records, the name of the marshal who recorded them.

Crash and error reports. If the app crashes or a sync fails, a technical report (error message, app version, device model and operating system version) is sent to Sentry, our error-reporting service, so we can fix problems. Sentry also receives anonymous performance timings from a sample of app sessions, and tags reports with a random identifier created for each installation of the app. These reports are not used for advertising or profiling.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any payment information. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Where your information is stored

  • Race and account data is stored with Supabase in Sydney, Australia.
  • Crash reports are stored with Sentry in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Organiser and marshal devices also keep a local copy of race data so timing works offline at race venues.

All data is encrypted in transit, and access to the club database is restricted by row-level security: participants can read published race results but cannot modify race data.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in club members can see published race results — that is the point of a club results app. Results include rider name, bib, times, laps and placing.
  • Club organisers and marshals can see the rider list and race records needed to run events.
  • We do not sell or share your information with any third party for marketing. The only third parties that process data are our service providers named above (Supabase and Sentry), acting on our behalf.

Children

The app is intended for club organisers, marshals and adult members. Junior riders may appear in race results as part of normal club record-keeping, under the club’s membership arrangements with their parent or guardian. Children are not expected to hold their own accounts.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Delete your account any time, in the app — see how to delete your account. This permanently removes your login and unlinks your rider profile. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to revoke its access to your Apple ID.
  • Race results are the club’s historical record and are kept after an account is deleted, in the same way paper results would be. If you want your name removed from published results as well, email adminrockyriders@gmail.com and we will action it.
  • You can also request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at the same address.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced through the club.
