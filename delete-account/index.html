Delete your Rocky Riders account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time. It takes effect immediately.

In the app

  1. Open Rocky Riders and sign in.
  2. Go to the right place for your account type:
    • Riders (participants): your participant page → Delete my account
    • Organisers: Home → menu (☰) → Delete Account
    • Marshals: Marshal Mode → Delete my account
  3. Confirm twice. You are signed out and your account is gone.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email address, name and sign-in details) — deleted permanently.
  • The link between your account and your rider profile — removed.
  • If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to revoke its access to your Apple ID.

What is kept

Published race results are the club’s historical record and are kept, like paper results. Crash and problem reports (including their masked screen recordings and screenshots) expire automatically at our error-reporting provider. To have your name removed from published results too, email adminrockyriders@gmail.com.

Can’t open the app?

Email adminrockyriders@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Rocky Riders account”, and we will delete it for you.
