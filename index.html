Rocky Riders

The club app of the Castlemaine Rocky Riders mountain bike club

Rocky Riders runs race day for the club: organisers create races and check riders in, marshals record riders at checkpoints (even with no phone signal), and riders see their published results, lap times and section splits on their own phone.

For riders

  • Create a free account and link it to your rider profile
  • See your race history: placings, points, lap times and DNFs
  • Browse published results for recent club races

For organisers and marshals

  • Calibration, Pursuit (handicap start) and Enduro (checkpoint) race formats
  • Marshal Mode works offline on the trail and uploads when there's signal
  • Results are only published once an organiser has reviewed them

Organiser and marshal access is by invitation from the club.
